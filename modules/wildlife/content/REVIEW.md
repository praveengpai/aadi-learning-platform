# Content needing human review (wildlife module)

1. **Compensation process and timelines** — the claim steps, the 14-day "claim may be late" window
   (`WILDLIFE_CLAIM_WINDOW_DAYS`), required documents and how amounts are assessed. The app never states
   a deadline or an amount as fact; confirm with the Forest Department before a real pilot.
2. **Helplines** — only 112 (national emergency number) is shown. Add the local range office / rapid
   response team numbers per hamlet once verified (catalogue `helpline.note` says PENDING REVIEW).
3. **Animal list** — elephant, wild boar, gaur, monkey, peacock, porcupine, leopard/tiger, snake, other.
   Check with communities and the range office (e.g. sambar, Nilgiri langur, bear) and whether snakebite
   belongs here or with health.
4. **Malayalam** — all labels, plain-language claim steps, sighting sentences ("… ഭാഗത്ത് ആനകളെ കണ്ടു")
   and the early-warning message were written by the build team; a native speaker should check them,
   and the tribal languages/dialects of each hamlet (Muthuvan, Irula …) may need audio rather than text.
5. **Safety advice** — the elephant-warning wording ("avoid forest paths after dark, keep children close")
   and the injury message should be checked by the range office and a medical officer.
6. **Readings** — ranges for pH and turbidity strips, and whether stream level should alert on floods.
